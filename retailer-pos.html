POS ng Bayan by JLD

A web-based point-of-sale and business management app for small and growing businesses. It works offline, syncs when you’re back online, and shows you at a glance whether your business is thriving or falling.

Type
Web application, works on any device
My role
Design and development
Access
Sign-in only. No public demo, to protect business data
Visual style
Deep navy and blue, the same palette used across this site

The problem it addresses

Small retail businesses often run sales, stock, and records across notebooks, spreadsheets, and memory. That makes it hard to see what’s happening day to day, and easy for mistakes to slip in.

POS ng Bayan by JLD is my attempt at putting the everyday work of a small business in one place: built around how a shop actually operates, simple enough to manage yourself, and affordable for people just starting out.

What’s in it

  • Works offline and syncs. Keep working without a connection; your data syncs once you’re back online.
  • Income and expense dashboard. Track your overall income and expenses in one view.
  • Know how the business is doing. See at a glance whether your business is thriving or falling.
  • Tax-report ready. Records are organized so your tax reports are ready when you need them.
  • Different use for different members. Each type of member gets the access and tools that fit their role.
  • Business-based sign-in and owner-controlled accounts. Each business works in its own space, and the owner decides who gets an account.
  • Easy to manage and easy to adjust. It can be modified to fit the way your business runs.
  • Budget friendly. Good for starting a business without paying for heavy software.
  • Any device. Use it on a phone, tablet, or computer.
  • Password recovery. A “Forgot password?” flow for users who lose access.

I only list features that are working. More screenshots and detail will be added as I document the project.
