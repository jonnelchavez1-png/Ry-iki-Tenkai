Project overview

AnyJuanCLERK removes manual data entry from paperwork. Staff upload documents, and it reads them, cleans the data, puts each value in the right field and exports the result. A person only checks the values it is unsure about, instead of typing every record.

It works in three steps: Upload, Review and Your results. In Review, the original document is on the left and the extracted values are on the right, and only the fields that need a look are shown. Doubtful values are marked amber, missing values red, and nothing is guessed. Each value goes into one field only.

It is built to keep documents with you. No documents are saved in a database. Files are held only temporarily while you work, and they stay on your own device. It reads without any AI connection, using free OCR on your own computer. An optional AI double-check is part of the design, but it is not connected yet.

What it can do

  • Reads many kinds of files: PDFs, photos, scans, Word, Excel, CSV and text files, several at once.
  • Free OCR: photos and scans are read on your own computer. No AI key is needed.
  • Cleans the data: phone numbers in the international format, dates as YYYY-MM-DD, and tidy capitalisation of names and cities.
  • Reads the details you choose: type any detail, such as “Policy No.”, or pick one from a document you opened.
  • Learns from you: Teach lets you show it where a value sits once. Saved profiles remember that for each kind of document.
  • Exports: to Excel or CSV, adds rows to your own spreadsheet, or fills Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF and text templates.
  • Keeps a record: a results ledger of what was read, what was corrected and who approved it, a check for duplicate records, and an optional login.

How it works

Each file goes through the same steps, in this order:

  1. Exact text is taken from the file, if it has any (digital PDFs and Word files).
  2. Free OCR reads photos and scans on your own computer.
  3. Rules and a placement step match labels and value shapes to fields.
  4. Checks catch impossible values, such as a birth date in the future.
  5. An optional AI second opinion is planned as the last step and is not connected yet. It can never override exact text or something you taught the app.

Who it helps

  • Data-entry and back-office teams in utilities, telcos, insurance, lending, clinics, HR and BPOs.
  • Reviewers, who check only the flagged fields instead of typing every record.
  • Small and privacy-conscious organisations, since documents stay on their own machines.
  • Managers and auditors, who get a record of what was read, corrected and approved.

Where it stands

Working

This tool is working and still being improved. 33 automated test suites pass. On generated scans the reader got 264 of 264 fields right, and a placement test put a wrong value in none of 132 fields.

Limits to know about: those tests use documents I generated, so accuracy on real documents is still being measured. Handwriting is weak without AI, so the app flags it instead of guessing. The defaults suit Philippine phone formats and addresses. For a demo or trial, email me.
