FinFlow

A financial automation and management system that helps small businesses streamline their financial operations through automated record collection, centralized data management and intelligent financial monitoring.

Status
Working
Type
Financial automation and management system
Built with
Telegram Bots, Google Workspace (Google Apps Script), Gemini AI and Google Sheets

Project overview

FinFlow connects Telegram Bots in business group chats, Google Workspace powered by Google Apps Script, and Gemini AI, which extracts, cleans and organizes the data. A web dashboard connects directly to the central Google Sheets data and turns submitted records into structured information, financial summaries and reports.

Authorized team members submit receipts and transaction records in a Telegram group chat. The system processes and organizes them and consolidates everything into one central database, which reduces manual encoding and repetitive admin work.

By combining group collaboration, AI-assisted processing, workflow automation and centralized reporting, FinFlow simplifies bookkeeping, improves recordkeeping and helps business owners make informed decisions.

What it tracks

Sales, income, expenses, payments, taxes, discounts, service charges and other transactions. Owners monitor cash flow, revenue, expenses, profitability and overall performance on the dashboard.

How it works

  1. Authorized team members submit receipts and transaction records in the business’s Telegram group chat.
  2. The system processes each record, and Gemini AI extracts, cleans and organizes the data.
  3. The records are consolidated in a central Google Sheets database.
  4. The web dashboard reads that data and shows structured information, financial summaries and reports.
