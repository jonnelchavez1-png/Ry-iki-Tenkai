Project overview

AnyJuanMEET is a web-based scheduling platform where guests can check available dates and time slots, choose a meeting duration, and submit booking requests. The host can review, approve, or decline requests through an admin dashboard, while guests receive email updates and a link to manage, reschedule, or cancel their bookings.

The system also includes calendar-based booking management, availability controls, and date blocking to make scheduling easier and more organized. It works like a Calendly-style booking page, built around a request-and-approve flow, so the host stays in control of who gets a slot.

It is working today and I am still improving it, with more features and polish planned.

What it can do

For guests

  • See live availability on a month calendar and pick a day and time.
  • Choose a meeting length: 15, 30 or 60 minutes.
  • Send a request with a name, email, optional phone number and an optional note. No verification email is needed.
  • Get email updates when the request is approved or declined, plus a link to manage, reschedule or cancel the booking.
  • Use light or dark mode. Times are shown in the host’s time zone.

For the host

  • See totals for bookings, requests awaiting approval, confirmed meetings and today’s meetings.
  • Approve or decline requests, and search or filter all bookings by guest or status.
  • View every booking on a calendar, colour-coded as confirmed, awaiting approval, blocked or other.
  • Block single dates or ranges for holidays and days off, so they cannot be booked.

How it works

  1. A guest opens the booking page and picks a meeting length, a date and a time.
  2. They add their details and send the request.
  3. The host reviews it in the admin dashboard and approves or declines it.
  4. The guest gets an email with the result and a link to manage, reschedule or cancel.
