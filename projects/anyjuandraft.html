Project overview

AnyJuandraft turns rough notes into work that is ready to send. It has four tools: Handover, Compose, Documents and File-to-Report. They share one set of saved defaults, so names, contacts and wording are filled in for you each time.

Handover is the main one. You paste messy notes, using @name for owners, “by Friday” for due dates, and lines like Urgent, Pending and Done for sections. It finds the tasks, groups them by urgency, and writes a task endorsement with a clear table. It also warns you about things like a task that falls due while you are away.

It is built to keep your information with you. Everything is stored only in your browser. There are no accounts and no analytics, and files you add are read in your browser and are never stored. The one outside request is the open-source pdf.js reader, which loads only when you open a PDF.

What it can do

  • Handover: turn notes into a task endorsement, with owners, due dates, status and priority, or into an out-of-office message with separate wording for inside and outside your organisation.
  • Compose: write an email, follow-up, reminder, request, approval request, announcement, apology, clarification, escalation, acknowledgment, decline, confirmation or a custom message, with a live preview and another wording on request.
  • Documents: meeting minutes, task handover, business report, progress report, incident report, and daily or weekly update. Rough notes are sorted into the right sections.
  • File-to-Report: add a CSV, Excel, Word or text-based PDF file and get a report with an overview, key figures and a category breakdown.
  • Settings: save your defaults once, including Cc, cover contact, default owner, title, subject pattern and opening line.
  • Output: copy for Outlook, copy as text, print, or download. Messages can also be saved as drafts or templates.

How it works

  1. Pick a tool: Handover, Compose, Documents or File-to-Report.
  2. Paste your notes or add your files.
  3. Check the result on the right. You can edit it directly.
  4. Copy, print or download it, and send it yourself.
