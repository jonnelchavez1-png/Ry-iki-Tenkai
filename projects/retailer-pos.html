Featured project

Retailer POS

A web-based point-of-sale and business management app for retailers, where each business signs in to its own space and the owner decides who gets an account.

Type
Web application
My role
Design and development
Access
Sign-in only. No public demo, to protect business data
Visual style
Deep navy and blue, the same palette used across this site

The problem it addresses

Small retail businesses often run sales, stock, and records across notebooks, spreadsheets, and memory. That makes it hard to see what’s happening day to day, and easy for mistakes to slip in.

Retailer POS is my attempt at putting the everyday work of a retail business in one place, built around how a shop actually operates.

What’s in it

  • Business-based sign-in. Users sign in to their own business, so each shop works in its own space.
  • Owner-controlled accounts. Accounts are created by the business owner, not by open sign-up, so access stays under the owner’s control.
  • Password recovery. A “Forgot password?” flow for users who lose access.

More detail on the sales and business-management features is being added as I document the project. I only list what is working.
