Featured project

AnyJuanPOS

A web-based point-of-sale and business management app for small and growing businesses. It works offline, syncs when you’re back online, and shows you at a glance whether your business is thriving or falling. It isn’t only for retailers: it can be modified to fit the business it serves.

Type
Web application that can also be installed as an app (PWA), including on a desktop, and keeps working offline
My role
Design and development
Access
On mobile, tablet, PC or laptop. Sign-in only, so there is no public demo, to protect business data. Want a trial or to test it? Please let me know.
Visual style
Deep navy and blue, the same palette used across this site. It can be changed to match your brand.

The problem it addresses

Small businesses often run sales, stock, and records across notebooks, spreadsheets, and memory. That makes it hard to see what’s happening day to day, and easy for mistakes to slip in.

AnyJuanPOS is my attempt at putting the everyday work of a small business in one place: built around how a business actually operates, simple enough to manage yourself, and affordable for people just starting out. Shops are the obvious fit, but it isn’t limited to retail.

What’s in it

  • Works offline and syncs. Keep working without a connection; your data syncs once you’re back online.
  • Income and expense dashboard. Track your overall income and expenses in one view.
  • Know how the business is doing. See at a glance whether your business is thriving or falling.
  • Tax-report ready. Records are organized so your tax reports are ready when you need them.
  • Different use for different members. Each type of member gets the access and tools that fit their role.
  • Business-based sign-in and owner-controlled accounts. Each business works in its own space, and the owner decides who gets an account.
  • Easy to manage, easy to adjust. It isn’t only for retailers. It can be modified based on your business and how you work.
  • Budget friendly. Good for starting a business without paying for heavy software.
  • Any device. Open it on a mobile phone, tablet, PC or laptop, or install it as an app that works offline on a desktop.
  • Password recovery. A “Forgot password?” flow for users who lose access.

I only list features that are working. More screenshots and detail will be added as I document the project.
