Telegram-Google Workspace Automation (TEGOWA)

A Telegram group chat that works like a bookkeeping desk. Team members post receipts, AI reads them, the bot flags anything that needs review, and approved records feed a live dashboard.

Project overview

Telegram-Google Workspace Automation (TEGOWA) is a financial automation and management system for small businesses. It cuts down manual encoding and repetitive admin work by collecting receipts and transaction records automatically, keeping them in one central place, and keeping an eye on the numbers for the owner.

It brings together a Telegram Bot in the business’s group chat, Google Workspace powered by Google Apps Script, and Gemini AI, which reads, cleans and organizes the data. Authorized team members post a receipt or record in the chat. The receipt itself is saved in Google Drive, and together with Google Sheets it serves as the business’s database.

The bot checks every record and tells the team when something looks wrong or needs review, before it is approved or goes any further. It catches duplicate photos, dates that are too old, totals that do not add up, and merchants that do not match the business. Details can be fixed right in the chat with short commands, and a record is only counted in reports once it has been approved.

A web dashboard reads the Google Sheets data and turns the approved records into financial summaries and reports. Owners can follow cash flow, revenue, expenses, profitability and overall performance, which makes bookkeeping simpler and helps them make informed decisions.

What it tracks

Sales, income, expenses, payments, taxes, discounts, service charges and other transactions.

How it works

  1. An authorized team member posts a receipt or transaction record in the business’s Telegram group chat.
  2. Gemini AI reads the receipt and organizes the details. The receipt is saved in Google Drive, and the details go into Google Sheets, which together serve as the database.
  3. The bot checks the record and tells the team about duplicates, discrepancies or anything that needs review before it is approved.
  4. An authorized member fixes any details, then taps Approve or Reject. Only approved records are counted in reports.
  5. The web dashboard reads the Google Sheets data and shows summaries and reports.
