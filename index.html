01About

I like computers, systems, and work that runs smoothly.

02How I can help

The work behind the work.

Seven areas where my experience has been put to real use, and what each one solves for a business.

i.

Administrative & virtual assistance

Day-to-day admin work: keeping records and files in order, following up by email and fax, processing documents, and handling front-desk style requests. I’ve done this in government offices, insurance support, and corporate teams.

The problem
Small admin tasks pile up, and the ones nobody owns get dropped.
How it helps
I take ownership of the follow-ups, files, and requests so they keep moving, and you can spend your time on the work only you can do.
ii.

Data management & documentation

Data entry and encoding, proofreading records, turning scanned or printed paper into clean digital files, and building Excel reports and summaries. Hands-on with Excel (including PivotTables and basic VBA), Word, and Access.

The problem
Messy or inconsistent data leads to wrong decisions and rework.
How it helps
Accurate, well-organized records you can search, report on, and trust. I check my own work before it goes out.
iii.

Order & supply chain coordination

Following bulk orders from start to finish: coordinating retailers, warehouses, and logistics partners, chasing delays, and securing proof-of-delivery (POD) and bill-of-lading (BOL) documents in TMS and supply chain platforms.

The problem
Many parties touch one order, and information gets lost between them.
How it helps
One person follows each order through, so suppliers and customers get accurate updates and complete paperwork on time.
iv.

Due diligence, credentialing & risk review

Verifying documents, checking compliance, spotting discrepancies, and keeping clean records that support risk assessment. This is my current line of work, in support of sales teams.

The problem
Incomplete or inconsistent paperwork creates risk that shows up too late.
How it helps
Careful, documented checks that catch gaps early and keep every file audit-ready.
v.

Customer, sales & stakeholder support

Supporting customers and sales teams by phone, chat, and email: answering billing and order questions, giving status updates, handling disputes calmly, and escalating issues to the right team. Comfortable in CRM and contact-center tools such as Salesforce and Avaya.

The problem
Customers and partners need clear answers, and unresolved issues get expensive.
How it helps
Calm, clear communication, with every issue followed up or escalated instead of left open.
vi.

Digital tools, AI & automation

Working across ERP, TMS, CRM, and ticketing systems, using AI assistants (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot) to speed up routine work, and building small web tools and automations with Google Workspace and Apps Script.

The problem
Repetitive manual steps waste time and invite mistakes.
How it helps
Simpler workflows and, where it makes sense, a purpose-built tool, so the task is done once and done right.
vii.

Healthcare & insurance claims support

Claims filing and validation, policy reviews, billing questions, and order status follow-ups. I’ve coordinated with hospitals, doctors, and internal teams, and reviewed claim forms and invoices for completeness, justification, and authenticity before reimbursement.

The problem
Claims stall when documents are missing or details don’t add up, and customers and providers are left waiting.
How it helps
I review each claim carefully, request what’s missing from the right party, and keep everyone updated until it’s resolved.

06My approach

What working with me looks like.

I pay attention to the details

Reviewing insurance claims and running due diligence checks trained me to read every document closely and flag what doesn’t add up before it becomes a problem. I proofread my own work before it goes out.

I follow things through

When an order is late or a document is missing, I track it, chase it, and escalate it to the right people instead of assuming someone else will.

I think in processes

Coordinating retailers, warehouses, 3PLs, and logistics partners meant seeing how every step connects. I look for where work slows down and what would make it simpler.

I learn the tool, then improve with it

I’ve worked in more than a dozen business systems and picked up AI tools to speed up routine work. Because of my IT background, I can also build a small tool myself when that’s the better answer.

I’m an AI and automation enthusiast

I like finding where AI and automation can take repetitive work off people’s plates. I use assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot, and I build small tools and automations when a task keeps coming back.

I keep learning

I keep up with AI and automation through training, e-learning courses, and videos, then test what I learn on real tasks so it becomes something useful, not just something I watched.

I communicate clearly

Years of calls, chats, and emails with US customers and with different parties such as landlords, 3PLs, hospitals, and doctors taught me to explain things plainly, set expectations, and keep people updated until the issue is closed.

I handle sensitive information with care

HIPAA-protected information, claims, invoices, POD billing, credentials, and client records aren’t things to be casual about. I keep confidential information confidential, which is also why details about past employers and clients are shared on request.
