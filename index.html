01About

I like computers, systems, and work that runs smoothly.

I’m Jonnel, 27, born and raised in Antipolo City. I’ve always enjoyed working with computers and systems, and that curiosity steered me into operations and support roles.

I started as a data encoder at 18, working to support myself through college, and I’ve kept growing since: data assistant, customer support, supply chain coordination, and now due diligence, credentialing and risk review. That adds up to more than six years in BPO and shared services, mostly with US clients, across healthcare, supply chain, logistics, and telecom.

What that gave me is a feel for how work actually moves through a business: where documents get stuck, where information goes missing, and where a small fix saves everyone time. I use that when I support a team, and I’ve started building small tools of my own to fix the same kinds of problems.

I’m looking for international remote work that offers growth and stability: in-house with a shared-services team, directly with a client, or on a freelance project.

02How I can help

The work behind the work.

Six areas where my experience has been put to real use, and what each one solves for a business.

i.

Administrative & virtual assistance

Day-to-day admin support: records, files, email and fax follow-ups, document processing, and front-desk style assistance.

The problem
Admin tasks pile up, and the small ones get dropped.
How it helps
Someone dependable keeps files, requests, and follow-ups moving so you can focus on the work only you can do.
ii.

Data management & documentation

Data entry and encoding, checking and proofreading records, turning scanned or printed paper into clean digital files, and Excel reports and summaries.

The problem
Messy or inconsistent data leads to wrong decisions and rework.
How it helps
Accurate, well-organized records that can be searched, reported on, and trusted.
iii.

Order & supply chain coordination

Tracking bulk orders end to end, coordinating retailers, warehouses, and logistics partners, and securing proof-of-delivery and bill-of-lading documents.

The problem
Orders involve many parties, and information gets lost between them.
How it helps
One person following each order through, so suppliers and customers get accurate updates and paperwork on time.
iv.

Due diligence, credentialing & risk review

Verifying documents, checking for compliance, spotting discrepancies, and keeping accurate records to support risk assessment.

The problem
Incomplete or inconsistent paperwork creates risk that surfaces too late.
How it helps
Careful, documented checks that catch gaps early and keep the file audit-ready.
v.

Customer & stakeholder support

Inbound and outbound calls, chat and email support, billing and claims questions, order status, escalations, and coordination with internal teams and outside parties such as hospitals and doctors.

The problem
Customers and partners need clear answers, and unresolved issues get expensive.
How it helps
Calm, clear communication, with issues followed up or escalated to the right team instead of left open.
vi.

Digital tools & process support

Working across business systems (ERP, TMS, CRM, ticketing), using AI assistants to speed up routine work, and building small web tools for repetitive tasks.

The problem
Repetitive manual steps waste time and invite mistakes.
How it helps
Simpler workflows and, where it makes sense, a purpose-built tool so the task is done once and done right.

06My approach

What working with me looks like.

I check the details

Reviewing insurance claims and running due diligence checks taught me to read documents closely and flag what doesn’t add up before it becomes a problem.

I follow things through

When an order is late or a document is missing, I track it, chase it, and escalate it to the right people, instead of assuming someone else will.

I think in processes

Coordinating retailers, warehouses, and logistics partners meant seeing how every step connects. I look for where work slows down and what would make it simpler.

I learn the tool, then improve with it

I’ve worked in more than a dozen business systems and picked up AI tools to speed up routine work. Because of my IT background, I can also build a small tool myself when that’s the better answer.
