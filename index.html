01About

I like computers, systems, and work that runs smoothly.

I’m Jonnel, 27, born and raised in Antipolo City. I’ve always enjoyed working with computers and systems, and that curiosity steered me into operations and support roles.

I started as a data encoder at 18, working to support myself through college, and I’ve kept growing since: data assistant, customer support, supply chain coordination, and now due diligence, credentialing and risk review. That adds up to more than six years in BPO and shared services, mostly with US clients, across healthcare, supply chain, logistics, and telecom.

What that gave me is a feel for how work actually moves through a business: where documents get stuck, where information goes missing, and where a small fix saves everyone time. I use that when I support a team, and I’ve started building small tools of my own to fix the same kinds of problems.

06My approach

What working with me looks like.

I pay attention to the details

Reviewing insurance claims, tracking paperwork, and handling due diligence checks have taught me to look closely at documents and flag what doesn’t add up before it becomes a problem. I proofread my own work before it goes out.

I follow things through

When an order is delayed or a document is missing, I take ownership, follow up, and try to resolve it. If needed, I escalate it to the right people. I bring the same approach to everything I do.

I think in processes

Coordinating with retailers, warehouses, 3PLs, and other business partners taught me how each step connects and affects the next. I look for where work slows down and what can be done to make things simpler and smoother.

I learn from the machine, then improve with it

I’ve worked with different business systems and tools, learning how they work, what they’re used for, and how to get the most out of them. Over time, I started building my own tools and exploring AI to bring my ideas to life, simplify tasks, and work more efficiently. With my IT background, I can go a step further by creating simple tools to solve recurring problems and improve everyday workflows.

I’m an AI and automation enthusiast

I like finding where AI and automation can take repetitive work off people’s plates. I use assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot, and I build small tools and automations when a task keeps coming back.

I keep learning

I keep up with AI and automation through training, e-learning courses, and videos, then test what I learn on real tasks so it becomes something useful, not just something I watched.

I communicate professionally

Working with US customers, onshore and offshore teams, and various partners, from retailers and suppliers to landlords, 3PLs, hospitals, and doctors, has taught me to understand different communication styles, adjust my tone, set expectations, and keep everyone on the same page until issues are resolved.

I handle sensitive information with care

HIPAA-protected information, claims, invoices, POD billing, credentials, and client records aren’t things to be casual about. I keep confidential information confidential, which is also why details about past employers and clients are shared on request.
